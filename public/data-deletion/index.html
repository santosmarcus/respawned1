Delete your data

ResPAWnd · Last updated 20 November 2026

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open ResPAWnd and sign in.
  2. Go to your Profile (organization staff: open the menu, then Account).
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion happens straight away. A few accounts have to hand over first: an organization's owner or only Admin, and a transporter with a trip in progress. The app tells you what to do.

Can't open the app?

Email respawnd.mgmt@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my ResPAWnd account". We delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, phone, saved location and notification tokens.
  • Your sign-in account.
  • Your volunteer and transporter applications, organization membership and pending invitations.
  • Any organization sign-up you started but didn't submit.

What is kept, without your name

  • Rescue reports you filed: the animal's case history stays with the rescue, but your name and phone number are removed.
  • Donation and adoption records, which organizations must keep for their finances and the animal's history.

Facebook

ResPAWnd uses Facebook only for organizations that connect their Facebook Page. To remove that connection, an organization admin can tap Disconnect under Organization Profile → Facebook Page Integration, which deletes the Page token we hold. You can also remove ResPAWnd in Facebook under Settings → Business Integrations. Posts already published stay on the Page until the Page deletes them.
