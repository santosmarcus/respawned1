Privacy Policy

ResPAWnd · Last updated 20 November 2026

ResPAWnd is an app for reporting animals that need rescue, following rescues, adopting, sponsoring and volunteering, and for the animal welfare organizations that run them. This page explains what the app collects, why, who sees it, and how to have it deleted.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number and sign-in method (email and password, or Google). Organizations also give their name, address, contact details and verification documents.
  • Rescue reports: what you report about an animal: photos and videos, its condition, the place it was found, and your contact details so the rescue team can reach you. Photos may carry the time and place they were taken.
  • Location: your location when you file a report or use the map. If you allow location in the background, the app saves your approximate last location to alert you about rescue reports near you; pet transporters share their location during a trip so the organization can follow it. You can turn location off in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Adoption, volunteer and transporter applications: the answers and documents you submit (for example home details, IDs or licences asked for by the organization), and interview schedules.
  • Donations and sponsorships: the amount, what it supports, and payment status. Card and e-wallet details are entered on PayMongo's payment page and are never stored by ResPAWnd.
  • Device information: a notification token so we can send you alerts, and basic crash and usage information from the services listed below.

How we use it

  • To run rescues: send reports to the right organization, contact reporters, assign transporters and track each case.
  • To handle adoptions, sponsorships, donations and volunteer events, including receipts and follow-ups.
  • To send notifications you asked for, such as updates on your report or rescues near you.
  • To keep the service safe: verifying organizations and transporters, and keeping audit records of staff actions.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • The organization handling your case or application and its staff, limited by the permissions their organization gives them.
  • The public: only what is meant to be public. Animal profiles (name, photos and their descriptions, breed, age, a yes/no health summary) can be viewed and shared, including on the organization's Facebook Page. Public rescue reports never show the reporter's name or contact details, and surrender reports show only a rounded location.
  • Service providers that run parts of the app for us: Google Firebase and Google Cloud (accounts, database, file storage, notifications), Google Maps (maps, places and directions), PayMongo (payments), Meta/Facebook (when an organization connects its Facebook Page and posts animal profiles to it), and Zoom (adoption interviews).
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Facebook Page connection

Organization admins can connect their organization's Facebook Page so staff can post animal profiles to it. ResPAWnd uses Facebook Login only for that: it reads the list of Pages the admin manages and keeps an access token for the chosen Page, stored encrypted on our servers and never in the app. We post only what staff choose to share. Disconnecting the Page in Organization Profile deletes that token; you can also remove ResPAWnd from your Facebook settings under Business Integrations.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, applications and membership are deleted. Rescue case histories, donation and adoption records are kept because the animal's history and the financial records belong to the rescue, but your name and contact details are removed from them. See how to delete your data.

Your choices

  • View and edit your details in the app under your profile.
  • Turn notifications and location access on or off in your phone's settings.
  • Delete your account at any time: Profile → Delete account (staff: Account → Delete account).
  • Ask us what we hold about you, or to correct or delete it, by email (below).

Children

ResPAWnd is not meant for children under 13, and adoption and volunteer applications are for adults.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Cloud. Access inside an organization is limited by each staff member's permissions, and sensitive actions are logged.

Changes

We will update this page when what we collect or how we use it changes, and change the date above.
